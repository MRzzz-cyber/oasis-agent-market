# Prediction discovery and venue comparison

The prediction conversation card starts with six real Polymarket binary markets drawn from up to 100 active events ordered by 24-hour volume. It keeps one open Yes/No market per event, excludes elapsed deadlines, missing/empty advertised asks and player O/U props, and caps each topic at six to diversify the pool. The metadata ask is a discovery filter, not a verified executable quote. “换一批” rotates actual fetched records; it never invents an event or a probability. The public feed is cached up to 45 seconds and the visible discovery view polls every 60 seconds. This is polling, not a push/WebSocket feed. Search and topic shortcuts read existing public search endpoints.

Selecting a question or outcome opens venue comparison and automatically requests equal-share order-book estimates. The source market is fetched again by its platform ID. For Kalshi candidates, a public series catalogue selects at most three related series, each capped at 100 open non-combo markets; an unmatched series falls back to the bounded market scan. Search coverage and provider failures stay explicit. No match means none found in that scope, not no market anywhere.

Lexical relevance only finds candidates. Different or unknown deadlines, outcome labels and settlement definitions are displayed. Equivalence is always unverified; there is no automatic winner or total-cost ranking. Ask-depth subtotals exclude fees, gas and bridging; Polygon pUSD and Kalshi-account USD are displayed separately.

Users explicitly choose Polymarket before its existing in-site purchase card mounts. Discovering, comparing or choosing a venue never calls a wallet to sign. The purchase card independently prepares a user-reviewed executable budget, allowance and FOK order. Kalshi remains a link to its platform, with an account/funding requirement; this release does not add Kalshi signing or orders. Existing Oasis identity, journals, unknown-result holds and shared spending locks remain in place.

Changing the market, outcome, share quantity or wallet invalidates old estimates and purchase actions. Quote deadlines disable venue selection. Browsing away aborts public requests and disposes purchase listeners; it does not clear financial journals. Background feed refreshes pause during comparison, and no refreshed quote silently changes a purchase.

Validation uses synthetic browser fixtures with no real wallet operations at 1280px and 390px, including feed/shuffle, search, automatic comparison, one-provider outage, both unavailable, empty feed, explicit venue choice, quantity/outcome changes, return navigation, stale source and expired quote. Existing purchase browser fixtures and all repository tests remain required. Live smoke and production acceptance perform public GET requests only.
